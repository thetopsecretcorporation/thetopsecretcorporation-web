## Field Brief
If some users love it and most shrug, do not average them together. Find the people who love it and ask why.

A **wedge** is the narrow starting point where one customer type urgently wants one outcome. A **pivot** is changing a core bet because evidence says the current one is wrong.

## Why It Matters
Iteration is not adding features for everyone. That is how startups become beige.

A sharper wedge usually comes from narrowing the customer, pain, promise, product, sales path, or onboarding around the strongest pull.

## What to Do
### 1. Start with the strongest users
Look for customers who:
- Repeated the value event.
- Paid or renewed.
- Added teammates.
- Referred someone.
- Pushed through bugs.
- Said they would be very disappointed without it.
- Moved faster in sales because the pain was active.

### 2. Compare broad vs. sharp
**Broad:** "AI assistant for sales teams."  
**Sharp:** "Founder-led B2B SaaS teams selling $20k+ pilots who lose deals when discovery-call follow-up is late."

The sharp version tells you who to find, what hurts, and what to build first.

### 3. Diagnose the weak point
Use plain labels:
- **Customer:** wrong segment or buyer.
- **Problem:** pain too weak or rare.
- **Promise:** value unclear or unbelievable.
- **Product:** does not deliver the promised result.
- **Sales/onboarding:** right product, wrong path to value.

Examples:
- If only clinics with 5+ billers renew, stop selling to solo practices.
- If customers only use the weekly risk report and ignore the dashboard, cut the dashboard and make the report impossible to miss.

### 4. Choose one next experiment
Do not change ten things. Pick one:
- Narrow the ICP.
- Focus the problem.
- Rewrite the promise.
- Cut product scope.
- Fix onboarding.
- Change pricing or pilot package.
- Return to discovery.

Write what would make the experiment pass or fail.

## What Counts
You are done when the new wedge is more specific than the old one and tied to evidence, not hope.

Use this:

> We will focus on [specific customer] with [urgent trigger] who need [specific outcome], starting with [entry workflow], because [evidence of pull].
