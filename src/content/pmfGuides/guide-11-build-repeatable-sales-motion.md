## Field Brief
Sales is not repeatable because one founder can close weird deals by force.

A **sales motion** is the repeatable way you find, qualify, sell, and activate customers. It is repeatable when the same customer type keeps moving through the same path for the same reason.

## Why It Matters
Do not hire sales to discover the market. Hire sales after the founder can repeat the sale.

Early sales proves more than revenue. It proves you can find the buyer, get attention, qualify pain, navigate approval, deliver value, and do it again.

## What to Do
### 1. Define the target rule
A useful target rule includes stage, trigger, buyer, pain, and current workaround.

Example:

> Series A cybersecurity companies with 20-80 employees, hiring their first compliance lead, preparing for SOC 2, and using spreadsheets for vendor reviews.

That is a target. "Cybersecurity companies" is not.

### 2. Write one outreach message
Keep it specific and problem-led.

Example:

> Saw you are hiring a compliance lead and mention SOC 2. Teams at this stage usually manage vendor evidence in spreadsheets until the first audit gets painful. Worth comparing notes?

No pitch wall. No buzzword soup.

### 3. Qualify before counting the deal
**Qualification** means checking whether this prospect has the pain, authority, budget/path, timing, and reason to act.

Use five checks:
- Right customer.
- Pain now.
- Current workaround.
- Buyer or authority path.
- Next step that costs something.

Qualified opportunity example:

> Buyer joins, budget source named, security review scheduled, pilot success metric agreed.

Not qualified: "Founder had a great call."

### 4. Define stages by customer action
Do not create CRM stages based on your optimism.

Use stages like:
- Target identified.
- Reply received.
- Discovery completed.
- Pain confirmed.
- Buyer path confirmed.
- Pilot proposed.
- Commitment received.
- Activated to first value.
- Retained/expanded.

### 5. Capture the playbook
Document:
- Target list rules.
- Outreach language.
- Discovery questions.
- Qualification rules.
- Common objections and real answers.
- Pilot package.
- Follow-up cadence.
- Activation steps.
- Conversion evidence.

## What Counts
You are done when another smart person could attempt the same motion and not need the founder's charisma to understand it.
