## Field Brief
Growth does not fix a product people do not keep using. It just makes the leak louder.

**Retention** means customers keep using or paying after the first excitement fades.

**Premature scaling** means adding spend, hires, features, channels, or customer types before the core customer keeps getting value.

## Why It Matters
Do not scale to find product-market fit. Scale after the right customers retain, pay, refer, expand, and pull.

If retention is weak, paid acquisition buys churn. If sales is not repeatable, hiring salespeople spreads confusion. If the beachhead is not won, adjacent markets create a bigger mess.

## What to Do
### 1. Define retention for your product
Write:

> A retained customer repeats [value event] every [natural cadence] for [time window].

A daily workflow, monthly finance process, and annual compliance job need different retention windows.

### 2. Check the scale gate
You need evidence across four areas:
- **Retention:** the beachhead segment keeps using or paying.
- **Pull:** customers ask for more seats, referrals, volume, integrations, or expansion.
- **Repeatability:** acquisition and sales can be documented and repeated.
- **Capacity:** onboarding, support, reliability, and delivery can handle more customers.

### 3. Watch for false scale
False scale examples:
- 1,000 Product Hunt signups, but only 12 complete the core workflow twice.
- Revenue rises, but churn is unknown.
- Founder closes bespoke deals that require custom work every time.
- A new segment is interested but needs a different buyer, product, workflow, procurement path, and onboarding process.

Real readiness example:

> 25 independent clinics use the denial-review workflow every week, 18 renew after 90 days, 6 refer another clinic, and onboarding takes under two hours.

### 4. Choose one scaling lever
Do not turn every knob at once.

Pick one:
- More of the same outbound list.
- One paid channel test.
- One onboarding improvement.
- One sales hire after founder repeatability.
- One adjacent segment with the same workflow.
- One infrastructure fix that removes a real bottleneck.

Define the pass/fail metric before spending.

### 5. Do not confuse adjacent with easy
Selling to hospital systems because they have budget may sound smart. But if they require procurement, integrations, security review, a different buyer, and a different workflow, that is not expansion. That is a new business.

## What Counts
You are ready to scale when the same narrow customer keeps reaching value, keeps paying, pulls more from you, and can be acquired and onboarded without heroics.
