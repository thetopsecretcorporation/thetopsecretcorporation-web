## Field Brief
Discovery interviews are not pitch meetings.

A **discovery interview** is a conversation where you learn how the customer handles the problem today. The goal is not to hear "cool idea." The goal is to find out whether the customer has already behaved like the problem matters.

## Why It Matters
People are polite. They will praise ideas they will never use.

Past behavior is harder to fake. Look for what they tried, bought, hacked together, ignored, escalated, or complained about.

## What to Do
### 1. Write your assumptions before the calls
Capture your current guess for:
- ICP.
- Painful moment.
- Current workaround.
- Cost.
- Trigger.
- User, buyer, approver, blocker.
- What would prove you wrong.

### 2. Recruit 10+ people from the same narrow segment
Ten is not magic. It is just enough to start seeing repeated patterns instead of one-off noise.

### 3. Ask about the past, not the fantasy future
**Bad:** "Would you use an AI assistant for recruiting?"  
**Better:** "Tell me about the last time you had to review 100 applicants. What did you do first? Who helped? What got skipped?"

Strong prompts:
- "Walk me through the last time this happened."
- "What did you use?"
- "Can you show me the spreadsheet, ticket queue, email thread, report, or dashboard?"
- "What did this cost in time, money, risk, or stress?"
- "Who cared when it went badly?"
- "What did you try before?"

### 4. Do not pitch until the end
If you pitch early, the call becomes theater.

Use this opener:

> I am not selling anything today. I am trying to understand how this works now. Specifics are more useful than encouragement.

### 5. Log evidence, not vibes
Mark each note as:
- **Behavior:** something they did.
- **Artifact:** spreadsheet, report, email, dashboard, contract.
- **Cost:** time, money, risk, delay, stress.
- **Quote:** exact customer language.
- **Opinion:** useful, but weak.

## What Counts
Weak outcome: "They said they would definitely use it."

Strong outcome: "They showed the spreadsheet, named the budget owner, and asked to test it on next week's applicant batch."
