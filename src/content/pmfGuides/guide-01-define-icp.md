## Field Brief
Your first customer cannot be "small businesses," "creators," or "busy professionals." That is fog.

An **ICP** is the first exact customer type you are choosing to focus on. It is not your total market. It is the narrow group where calls, sales, product choices, and word of mouth can repeat.

A **beachhead** is the small first market where wins can stack. Pick one before you chase everyone.

**Weak:** "Dentists."  
**Stronger:** "Independent orthodontic practices with 3-8 chairs, using Ortho2, with one office manager manually chasing no-show follow-ups after school hours."

That is usable. You can name them. You can find them. You can ask about the same workflow 25 times.

## Why It Matters
Broad customer definitions create fake learning. One call says price matters. Another says integrations. Another says design. You start averaging people who should never be in the same room.

A tight ICP makes every next step sharper: interviews, pain, offer, MVP, pricing, sales, and retention.

## What to Do
### 1. List 3-5 possible first customer types
For each one, write:
- Who they are.
- What painful workflow they share.
- What trigger makes it urgent now.
- Where you can find 25 real prospects without ads.
- Who uses, buys, approves, and blocks the product.

### 2. Score each segment fast
Use five filters:
- **Pain now:** do they already feel the problem?
- **Reach:** can you manually contact them?
- **Same workflow:** will one interview teach you about the next?
- **Ability to act:** can someone approve time, access, or budget?
- **Fast learning:** can you get answers this week?

### 3. Write exclusion rules
Exclusions protect focus.

Example: "We are not starting with national orthodontic chains. Corporate approval, procurement, multi-location rollout, and security review make that a different sale."

### 4. Build the first 25-prospect list
Each prospect should include:
- Name/company.
- Why they fit.
- Likely painful trigger.
- Contact path.
- User/buyer guess.

## What Counts
You are done when you can say:

> We are starting with [specific customer] who [specific painful workflow/trigger], because we can reach them manually, they share the same problem, and they can commit time, access, or budget.

If you cannot name 25 real prospects, the ICP is still too vague.
